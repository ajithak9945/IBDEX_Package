IBDEX

IBD Molecular Endotype Explorer

IBDEX projects intestinal bulk RNA-seq samples into a frozen, batch- and tissue-debiased latent reference learned from 3,168 harmonised IBD samples across four cohorts, and surfaces decoder-native pathway activity scores alongside the discovered colon and ileum endotypes.

Open Explorer →
Research use only. IBDEX is a research tool for exploring molecular endotype structure in IBD transcriptomic data. It is not a diagnostic device and is not validated for clinical decision-making. All data loaded into the explorer is processed locally in your browser and is never transmitted to any server.
Source and the ibdex-projector command-line tool: github.com/ajithak9945/IBDEX_Package
